Upcoming Events for Norhtville Seniors!

Prom Information:

Senior prom is coming up soon on May 21st! If you haven't already, make sure to buy your dress and your tickets. Tickets must be ordered by May 10th! This year prom will be held in the Fishbowl to go along with our Under the Sea theme. Best dressed will win a filly stocked fish tank and an unlimited subscription to Goldfish! Don't delay, get your tickets now!

When purchasing your ticket, keep in mond that each table only had ten seats available. This means a group of 5 can each bring along one guest. If you are planning on bringing a plus one, please specify that when you order their ticket.

Graduation Information:

Graduation is not too far away either! To continue the "Under the Sea" theme of this year, the graduation ceremony will be held ay Petosky Beach on May 31st at 7 pm. Please make sure to purchase any tickets for friends and family to watch the ceremony by May 10th so we can arrange seating.
