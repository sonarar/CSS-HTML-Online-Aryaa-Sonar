The Evolution of the Moving Image

Before cinema existed, optical toys amazed audiences by tricking the human eye into perceiving motion via sequential drawings.

Key Milestones in Animation History

  • 1832: Invention of the Phenakistoscope (spinning discs).
  • 1908: Fantasmagorie, the first fully animated cartoon film.
  • 1928: Disney's Steamboat Willie introduces synchronized sound.
  • 1995: Pixar releases Toy Story, the first feature-length computer-animated film.
