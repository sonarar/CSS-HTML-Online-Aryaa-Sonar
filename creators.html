Popular YouTube Aniamtion Community Creators

When I was younger, the animation community was thriving with lots of popular creators. Some of my personal favorites included:

Jaiden Animations

She is famous for her story time and Pokemon Nuzelock animations. As a huge Pokemon fan myself, I was a big fan of her content growing up.

Visit Jaiden Animation's Official YouTube Channel

Swoozie

Swoozie was one of the pioneers of the YouTube animation community. Some of my favorites out of his videos are the ones where he talks about his experience working at Disney Universal Studios.

Visit Swoozie's Official YouTube Channel
