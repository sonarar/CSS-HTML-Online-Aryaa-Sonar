How to Make an Origami Jumping Frog

A Step-by-Step Tutorial with Video Embedding

Fold Instructions

Step 1: Start with Square Paper

Place your square origami paper flat on the table. If it has a colored side, place the colored side facing down.

Step 2: Fold in Half Vertically

Fold the paper in half from left to right, crease it well, and then unfold it back to the start.

Step 3: Fold in Half Horizontally

Fold the top half of the paper down to meet the bottom edge. Crease firmly.

Step 4: Fold Corners to Create an X

Take the top right corner of the upper square and fold it diagonally to the left. Unfold it, then repeat with the top left corner to create an "X" crease pattern.

Step 5: Collapse into a Waterbomb Base

Push the sides of the "X" creases inward toward each other, collapsing the top section down into a neat triangle shape.

Step 6: Fold Up the Front Legs

Take the bottom corners of the top triangle and fold them upwards toward the top point to form the frog's front legs.

Step 7: Fold Sides Inward

Take the left and right vertical edges of the remaining bottom rectangle and fold them inward toward the center crease line.

Step 8: Fold Bottom Up

Fold the entire bottom edge of the paper upward so it touches the bottom of the triangle legs.

Step 9: Fold Corners Down

On that same bottom flap, fold the left and right inner corners diagonally outward to create a boat-like shape.

Step 10: Pull Flaps Down for Back Legs

Pull the inner points down and outward to form the shape of the back legs, flattening the creases securely.

Step 11: Angle the Back Legs

Fold the back legs outward slightly at an angle so they look like realistic, jumping hind legs.

Step 12: Create the Spring Fold

Fold the entire bottom half of the frog's body upward right along its waist line.

Step 13: Fold Back Down to Finish

Fold the top layer of that flap back down toward the tail end. This creates a "Z" shape accordion fold which serves as the jumping spring!
