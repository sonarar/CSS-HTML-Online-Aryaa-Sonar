Northville Senior News - Upcoming Events

Upcoming Events for Northville Seniors!

Prom Information:

Senior prom is comping up soon on May 21st! If you haven't already, make sure to buy your dress and order your tickets. Tickets must be ordered by May 10th! This year prom will be held in the Fishbowl to go along with the Under the Sea theme. Best dressed will win a fully stocked fish tank and an unlimited subscription to Goldfish! Don't delay, get your tickets now!

When purchasing your ticket, keep in mind that each table only has 10 seats available. This means a group of 5 can each bring along one guest. If you are planning on bringing a plus one, please specify that when you order their ticket.

Graduation Information:

Graduation is not too far away either, seniors! To continue the Under the Sea theme of this years, the graduation ceremony will be held at Petoskey Beach on May 31st at 7pm. Please make sure to purchase any tickets for friends and family to watch the ceremony by May 10th so we can arange seating.

Northville Student Council

Congrats to all the seniors and Go Mustangs!
